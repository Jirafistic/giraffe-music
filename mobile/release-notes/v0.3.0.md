**A YouTube Music client for Android that respects the platform's own interface.**
No ads, gentle on your battery, built on the [ytm-rs](https://github.com/grapedjuice/ytm-rs) Rust core.

## Download
| File | For |
|---|---|
| `giraffe-music-arm64-v8a.apk` | Almost every current phone |
| `giraffe-music-armeabi-v7a.apk` | Older 32-bit phones |
| `giraffe-music-universal.apk` | Not sure? This one works everywhere (bigger) |

Requires Android 8.0+. Android will ask you to allow installing apps from this source.

## Highlights
- 🎵 **The YouTube Music interface you know**: Home, Samples, Explore, Library, the 2025 player, queue, lyrics and related
- 🚫 **No ads**: plays the song's audio directly and skips non-music parts of music videos
- 🔋 **Battery first**: native player with audio offload, a 256 MB audio cache, nothing running in the background
- 👤 **Your account**: sign in with Google for your playlists, likes, history, personalized Home and synced searches
- ⬇️ **Downloads**: audio, artwork and lyrics, playable only inside the app
- 🎬 **Music videos**: synced with the song, released when you lock your phone
- 🔁 **Spotify import**: copy a public Spotify playlist to your YouTube Music library
- 🔄 **In-app updates**: future versions install from the app

## New in 0.3.0
- Undo when removing songs from the queue, playlists or likes
- Audio quality for mobile data and Wi-Fi, and Wi-Fi-only downloads
- Resume playback when headphones connect (optional)
- Time capsule: what you were listening to a week, a month or a year ago
- Pronunciation for Russian and Japanese (kana) lyrics
