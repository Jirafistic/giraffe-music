**A YouTube Music client for Android that respects the platform's own interface.**

## What's new
- ⬇️ **Download notification**: when you download songs, a notification shows the progress — *Downloading 23 of 257* — and tells you when everything is done. It keeps updating with the app closed or the screen off.
- 🔔 **Update notification**: Giraffe checks for new versions once a day in the background. When one is out you get a notification; tap it and the update screen opens.

The first time you open this version, Android asks for permission to show notifications.

## Update
From 0.3.x: the app offers this update on its own (or Settings → About → Check for updates).

New install? `giraffe-music-arm64-v8a.apk` (almost every current phone) or `giraffe-music-universal.apk` (works everywhere). Android 8.0+.
