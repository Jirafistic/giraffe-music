**A YouTube Music client for Android that respects the platform's own interface.**

## What's fixed
- 🖼️ **Missing covers**: playlists whose cover was a deleted or private video showed a broken image. Giraffe now retries with another version of the thumbnail, and if there is none it shows a gray placeholder, like YouTube Music.

## Update
If you have 0.3.0, the app will offer this update on its own: tap **Update** and it installs itself.

New install? Download `giraffe-music-arm64-v8a.apk` (almost every current phone) or `giraffe-music-universal.apk` (works everywhere). Requires Android 8.0+.
