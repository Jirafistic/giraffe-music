**A YouTube Music client for Android that respects the platform's own interface.**

## What's new
- ✨ **New interface**, like the latest YouTube Music: full-width covers on playlists and albums, a floating mini player, roomier lists with a ⋮ menu on every playlist, and a new font. The classic interface is still there: Settings → About → Interface (Old / New).
- 🔎 **Search inside a playlist**: the magnifier in a playlist, album, Liked Music or Downloads now finds songs in that list. If it isn't there, search everywhere with one tap.
- 🎤 **Tap a lyric to jump there**: in synced lyrics, tap a line and the song jumps to that moment.
- ➕ **Add songs from the playlist**: an "Add" row at the top of your playlists opens search; every song you tap is added.
- 🗑️ **Remove downloads of a playlist or album**: tap its download button (or "Remove downloads" in its ⋮ menu).
- ❌ **Songs YouTube no longer has** are skipped when downloading. When a playlist finishes, Giraffe offers to remove them; if you keep them, they're marked with an X.

## Fixed
- Downloads can no longer get stuck on a problematic song, and big playlists start downloading right away.
- The now-playing marker in lists follows the song that's actually playing.

## Update
From 0.4.x: the app offers this update on its own (or Settings → About → Check for updates).

New install? `giraffe-music-arm64-v8a.apk` (almost every current phone) or `giraffe-music-universal.apk` (works everywhere). Android 8.0+.
