**A YouTube Music client for Android that respects the platform's own interface.**

Patch release.

## Fixed
- Some songs that are available on YouTube Music (for example, from big labels) wouldn't play. Giraffe now tries other ways to get their audio.
- Adding songs to your playlists and removing songs from them now shows up right away.
- Removing the songs that weren't found after downloading a playlist now works.

## New
- 🖼️ **Playlist covers**: tap the cover on a playlist's edit screen to choose a photo, then move, zoom, and pick Fill or Fit (like choosing a profile picture). The cover is shown in Giraffe Music only.
- 🗑️ **Delete playlists** (also from your YouTube Music account): from the ⋮ menu in Library, inside the playlist, or from the edit screen.

## Update
From 0.5.0: the app offers this update on its own (or Settings → About → Check for updates).
