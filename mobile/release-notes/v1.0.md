**A YouTube Music client for Android that respects the platform's own interface.**

## Giraffe Music 1.0 🦒
- ✨ **Giraffe Expressive**: a completely redesigned interface in the Material 3 Expressive style that keeps YouTube Music's layout. Rounder shapes that change when you tap them, a light bounce, a bit of blur and the colors of the song that's playing. The play button changes shape while music plays, the time bar is thicker, settings are grouped in cards and menus slide in with a spring.
- 🎨 **Settings → Appearance**, a new section:
  - **Animations**: full (light bounce), soft or off. Off saves the most battery.
  - **Blur**: on or off.
  - **Corners**: round, medium or square.
- 🔎 **Search on regular YouTube too**: besides YouTube Music, results now include videos from regular YouTube (a "From YouTube" section and a **YouTube** chip), so covers, live versions and songs missing from YouTube Music are there. They play as audio like any other song: you can download them and add them to playlists.

Still battery-first: animations only move things on the GPU, nothing animates on its own, and blur is used only on a few small bars.

The Classic/New interface option is gone: Giraffe Expressive replaces both.

## Update
The app offers this update on its own (or Settings → About → Check for updates).

New install? `giraffe-music-arm64-v8a.apk` (almost every current phone) or `giraffe-music-universal.apk` (works everywhere). Android 8.0+. For Android Auto there's **Giraffe CarMusic** (`giraffe-carmusic-1.0.apk`).
