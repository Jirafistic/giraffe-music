**A YouTube Music client for Android that respects the platform's own interface.**

## New: Giraffe CarMusic 1.0 🚗
A second, lightweight app made for **Android Auto**: `giraffe-carmusic-1.0.apk`. It works on its own: search, your account, playlists and downloads.

- On the car screen: your library, playlists, downloads and search, with covers and the usual controls (play/pause, next, previous, seek bar, like, shuffle, repeat). Voice search works too.
- **Lyrics in the car**: the current line of the song shows where the artist's name usually goes (can be turned off).
- On the phone: a simple, fast player with the lyrics and the cover blurred in the background. Long-press a song, playlist or album to download it.
- **Linked with Giraffe Music**: if you have both (Giraffe Music 0.5.02 or newer), they share the account, the playlists and the downloads. A song downloaded in either app shows up in both and is stored only once.
- To see it in Android Auto: Android Auto settings → tap the version several times to enable developer mode → ⋮ → Developer settings → turn on **Unknown sources**.

## Update
Giraffe Music offers this update on its own (or Settings → About → Check for updates). Install CarMusic from the file above.
