**A YouTube Music client for Android that respects the platform's own interface.**

## What's new
- 👥 **Listen together**: tap the new person button in Library to create a session (QR + code) or join one. Up to 4 people on the same Wi-Fi hear the same song at the same moment, and anyone can play, pause or change it. A green bar shows who's connected.
- 🔎 **Search by lyrics**: type a piece of a song's lyrics (even with typos) and the song shows up as the top result, with the matching line.
- 🌐 **Spanish**: Giraffe now speaks Spanish. Settings → General → Language (follows your phone by default).
- 📝 **Playlists without an account**: create playlists before signing in; when you sign in, Giraffe offers to move them to your YouTube account.
- 🎧 **Audio quality like YouTube Music**: Wi-Fi always in high quality; on mobile data choose Auto (default), Low or High.
- ✨ **What's new**: after each update, short cards show what changed.
- 🎲 **Samples** now mixes your favorites with new recommendations and keeps going as you scroll.
- 👆 **Swipe the artwork** to change songs and see the next cover as you drag.

## Fixed
- Downloads no longer stop in the background: they keep going with the screen off, retry if the connection drops and continue where they left off if the app closes.
- Shuffle now mixes the whole queue, with the current song first, every time.
- Better performance in the player.

## Update
From 0.3.x: the app offers this update on its own (or Settings → About → Check for updates).

New install? `giraffe-music-arm64-v8a.apk` (almost every current phone) or `giraffe-music-universal.apk` (works everywhere). Android 8.0+.
